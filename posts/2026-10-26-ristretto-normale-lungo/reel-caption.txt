Same coffee, three espresso lengths. Watch what the water does to the cup.

In Italian, ristretto means restricted and lungo means long. Many cafés treat the standard shot, normale, as the middle point. Length is about the water, while single or double describes how much ground coffee goes in. A ristretto is planned to be short, not a normal shot cut off early.

If you could only order one length for the rest of the month, which would it be?
