Coffee is a crop, picked once a year in most places. The bag in your kitchen came from one harvest.

Beans from the year before are called past crop. Stored too long, or somewhere warm and humid, they can taste flat, papery or woody.

Have you ever spotted a harvest year printed on your coffee bag?

Sources: Specialty Coffee Association (sca.coffee); Green Coffee Collective (greencoffeecollective.com); Royal Coffee (royalcoffee.com); Coffee Review (coffeereview.com)
