The coffee in your kitchen came from one specific harvest, and that harvest has a year.

Once the next harvest arrives, beans from the year before are called past crop. Raw beans age slowly, and kept too long, or stored somewhere warm and humid, they can start to taste flat, papery or woody. How long they stay good depends a lot on storage, so there is no single cutoff.

Have you ever spotted a harvest year printed on your coffee bag?
