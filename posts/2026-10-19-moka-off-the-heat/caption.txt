That last gurgle from your moka pot is not a bonus. It is the signal to stop.

The coffee that comes out first is the good part. As the stream turns pale, the cup gets more bitter, and a hot pot keeps brewing even after you turn off the flame. Most guides say to take it off the heat at the first sputter, and some pull it even a little earlier.
