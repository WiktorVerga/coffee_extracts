A bitter shot is often a shot that ran too long, not a bad coffee.

Espresso (a small, concentrated coffee made under pressure) is a race between flavors. Sweet and acidic notes dissolve first, bitter ones later. Small changes to the grind move that balance more than you'd expect.

When your shot tastes bitter, what do you change first: the grind or the dose?
