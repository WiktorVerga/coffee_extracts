Ordering a macchiato? The word is about the stain, not the milk.

In Italian, macchiato means stained or marked. A caffè macchiato is espresso with a spot of milk on top. A latte macchiato flips it: mostly milk, marked with a little coffee. Menus vary, so look at the cup when it arrives.
