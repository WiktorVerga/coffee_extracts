Extra water in an espresso can go two ways: through the coffee, or on top of it.

In Italian the long one is a caffè lungo, and it leaves the machine already long. An americano starts as a regular shot and gets its hot water afterward. There's no fixed amount, so sizes vary from one café to the next. There's a third relative too: the long black, where the espresso is poured into the hot water instead.
