An americano and a lungo look the same in the cup. The extra water got there in two different ways.

A caffè lungo leaves the machine already long. An americano is a regular shot with hot water added afterward, and there's no fixed amount, so sizes vary.

Which one do you get when you ask for a "long" coffee where you live?
