Your moka pot is simple. Three small habits decide how good the cup is.

In many Italian homes, the moka is the stovetop pot behind the morning coffee. The recipe barely changes, so the details matter: where the water stops, how loosely the coffee sits, and how low the flame is. None of it needs extra gear.

Where does your water level stop: right at the valve, or a little below?
