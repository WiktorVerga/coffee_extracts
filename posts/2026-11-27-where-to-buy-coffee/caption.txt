A coffee bag can look fresh on the shelf and still tell you nothing about when the beans were roasted.

Coffee sold through big stores often needs a long shelf life, so the bag carries a best-before date and the roast date stays off. Smaller roasters tend to print a "roasted on" date so you can judge for yourself. And if a café grinds your beans, ask for a grind that suits your moka pot or espresso machine, then use it soon: ground coffee loses its freshness far faster than whole beans.
