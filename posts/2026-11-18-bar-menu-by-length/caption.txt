Most black coffees at an Italian bar line up on one scale: how much water meets the coffee.

The names do half the work, since ristretto means restricted and lungo means long. If you want the americano, ask for a caffè americano in full: in Italy, americano on its own is also the name of a cocktail. Cup sizes vary from bar to bar, so look at what arrives and adjust next time.
