Same bean, two very different cups. The roast alone can make one fruity and the other bittersweet.

Tostatura is the Italian word for roasting. Lighter roasts keep more acidity and fruit, darker ones lean roasty and bitter. The names on the bag are loose guides, so trust your cup.
