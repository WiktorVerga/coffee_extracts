Two bags, same bean. The roast alone can make one taste fruity and the other bittersweet.

Tostatura is the Italian word for roasting, and it is the step that turns a hard green seed into the coffee you brew. Darker roasts lean roasty and bitter, lighter ones keep more acidity and fruit. The names on the bag are loose guides, not exact measures.
