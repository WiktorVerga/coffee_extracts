Why is the crema on some espressos so thick? Robusta may be part of the answer.

Many espresso blends mix both species: arabica for sweetness and complexity, a little robusta for body and foam. Taste still varies a lot by farm, roast and processing.

Does your espresso bag say 100% arabica, or does it list robusta?
