Flying to Italy? One word on the bar menu will not get you the drink you ordered back home.

The English "latte" is a shortened caffellatte, from caffè e latte: coffee and milk. Drop the caffè and only the milk is left. In Italy, caffellatte is mostly a breakfast drink made at home, poured from a moka into warm milk and usually not foamed. At the bar, the milky glass you may be picturing is closer to a latte macchiato.
