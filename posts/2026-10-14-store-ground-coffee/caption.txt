Ground coffee has far more surface touching the air than beans do, so it fades fast. Here is how to slow that down.

Oxygen is the biggest factor in staling, with moisture and warmth close behind. A tight lid and a dark, cool cupboard do most of the work. Whether you brew in a moka pot or an espresso machine, the cup can only be as good as what is in the jar.
