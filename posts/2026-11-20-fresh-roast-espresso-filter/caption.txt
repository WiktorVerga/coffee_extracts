The freshest bag on the shelf isn't always the best one for your espresso machine.

Roast level plays a part too. Darker roasts usually let their gas out faster, while lighter roasts hold on to it longer, so they often need more rest. Roasters don't agree on exact rest times, so treat any number on the bag as a starting point and taste as you go.
