Decaf isn't brewed any differently. The caffeine is gone before the beans ever meet a roaster.

At many Italian bars you order it as a caffè decaffeinato, or just "deca". Behind that small word is a surprisingly physical process, done on raw beans. Each method trades cost against flavor in its own way, and sources don't fully agree on which one tastes best.
