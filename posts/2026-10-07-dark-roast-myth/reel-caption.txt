Dark roast tastes bolder. It doesn't mean more caffeine.

Roasting changes flavor far more than it changes caffeine. Scoops can mislead, because dark beans puff up and weigh less. Dose in grams and the two come out close.

Which do you reach for at home, light or dark, and what do you taste in it?
