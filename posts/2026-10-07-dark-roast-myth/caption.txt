Your dark roast tastes bolder. That doesn't mean it has more caffeine.

Roasting changes flavor far more than it changes caffeine. The big difference shows up when you measure by scoop: dark beans puff up and weigh less, so a scoop holds a bit less coffee. Weigh your dose in grams and the two come out close.
