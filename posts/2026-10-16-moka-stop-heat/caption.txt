Hear that hiss from your moka? That is your cue to lift it off the heat.

The last coffee out of a moka is pushed by steam, and it tends to taste bitter. Guides usually suggest stopping at the first gurgle, or even a touch earlier when the stream turns pale. Many also cool the base under cold water so the brewing stops right away. It depends on your pot and your stove, so taste and adjust.
