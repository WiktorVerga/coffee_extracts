The date most people check on a coffee bag tells you the least.

Many bags carry an expiry date set far ahead, which says little about taste. The roast date shows how old the beans really are. Freshly roasted coffee also needs a few days to release gas, so the best cup is usually a bit after roasting.

Which date do you look at first when you buy coffee?
