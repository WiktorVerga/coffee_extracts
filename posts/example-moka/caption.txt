Your moka pot forgives a lot. Not everything, though.

In Italy, the moka (that octagonal stovetop pot) is how most homes make coffee every morning. How you fill it matters more than the blend you buy. The step people skip most? Not tamping: in a moka, water needs an easy path through the coffee.

Do you fill yours right up to the valve, or stop a little below?
