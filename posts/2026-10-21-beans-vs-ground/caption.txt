Two bags, same coffee. The ground one loses its edge sooner, and surface area is why.

Once a bean is ground, far more of it touches the air, and oxygen is the main thing that makes coffee go stale. Whole beans keep that surface hidden until you grind. Pre-ground trades some of that freshness for speed, and that can be a fair trade on a busy morning.

Which one is in your kitchen right now: beans or pre-ground?

Sources: Specialty Coffee Association, "What is the Shelf Life of Roasted Coffee? A Literature Review on Coffee Staling" (sca.coffee)
