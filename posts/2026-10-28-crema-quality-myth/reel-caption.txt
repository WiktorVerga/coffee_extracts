That thick golden foam can come from the machine as much as from the coffee.

Crema is gas from the roast, and it can hint at freshness. But machines that force coffee through a tiny outlet whip air into it, so foam alone says little about flavor.

Does your machine make big, foamy crema, and does the cup live up to it?
