The coffee bean you grind every morning is not a bean at all.

In Italian it's a chicco, but botanically it's a seed, taken from the fruit of the coffee tree. Left unprocessed, that seed can be planted and grow into a tree. Drying, hulling and roasting turn it into the coffee in your cup.
