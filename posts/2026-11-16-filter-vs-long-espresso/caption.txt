A bigger cup doesn't turn an espresso into filter coffee. The water has to move differently.

In espresso, water is forced through a tight bed of fine coffee. In filter coffee it seeps down through the grounds with nothing pushing it. With a paper filter, some of the coffee's oils stay in the paper, which is why the cup comes out clear and light-bodied. Grinds and recipes vary, so taste and adjust.
