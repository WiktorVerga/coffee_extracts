A bitter shot usually isn't bad beans. It's water that stayed in the coffee too long.

Espresso flavor comes out in order: sweet and fruity first, bitter last. That's why a tiny change to the grind can turn a harsh cup into a balanced one. It also depends on the coffee, so taste and adjust.

When your espresso tastes bitter, which do you change first: the grind, the time, or the temperature?
