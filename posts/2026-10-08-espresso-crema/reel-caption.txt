That golden foam on your espresso is mostly gas. Here is what it can and can't tell you.

Roasting traps carbon dioxide in the beans. Pressure pushes it into the water, and coffee oils help hold the bubbles. Crema is a hint about freshness, not a verdict on flavor.

Do you judge your shot by the crema, or by the first sip?

Sources: Illy & Navarini, "Neglected Food Bubbles: The Espresso Coffee Foam", Food Biophysics 2011 (link.springer.com)
